# 📊 TELECOM CUSTOMER CHURN DASHBOARD | REGIONAL OVERVIEW

## 📌 Introduction

The **Telecom Customer Churn Dashboard – Regional Overview** provides a comprehensive view of customer churn, customer behavior, satisfaction, and revenue performance across different regions.

## 🎯 Key Areas Covered

* **Customer Overview** – Total customers, customer distribution, and customer trends.
* **Churn Analysis** – Churned customers, churn rate, and year-over-year churn performance.
* **Churn Drivers** – Analysis of factors influencing customer churn, including service plans, customer characteristics, and behavioral patterns.
* **Customer Satisfaction** – Satisfaction levels and their relationship with customer retention and churn.
* **Revenue Analysis** – Revenue contribution, revenue trends, and potential revenue impact from customer churn.
* **Regional Analysis** – Comparison of customer, churn, satisfaction, and revenue metrics across regions.
* **Trend Analysis** – Year-over-year and period-based trends to identify changes in customer behavior and business performance.

## 🔍 Dashboard Objective

The primary objective of this dashboard is to transform customer and business data into **actionable insights** that can support:

* Early identification of churn risks
* Understanding of key churn drivers
* Improvement of customer satisfaction and retention
* Identification of high-value customers at risk of churn
* Evaluation of revenue exposure due to churn
* Regional performance comparison
* Data-driven customer retention strategies

## 💡 Business Value

The regional overview provides a high-level starting point for identifying areas of concern, while the detailed analysis can be used to drill down into specific customer segments and churn drivers.

## 🛠️ Technology

* **Power BI**
* **DAX**
* **Power Query**
* **Data Modeling**
* **Interactive Data Visualization**

## 📈 Key Metrics

The dashboard includes metrics such as:

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Churn Rate
* Revenue
* Revenue at Risk
* Customer Satisfaction
* Churn by Region
* Churn by Service Plan
* Churn Driver Analysis
* Year-over-Year Performance
